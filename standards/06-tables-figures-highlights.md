# 06 Tables, figures and highlights

Source: DFI 5.1 1.2.25 (Table 1-2), 4.4.11 (Heading 5), 4.4.18–4.4.20,
Glossary (colours).

## 1. Tables and figures

| Rule | Tag | Cite |
|---|---|---|
| Refer to every table and figure in the body text by its number and title. | [M] | 1.2.25a |
| Centre tables and figures on the page. Nothing may extend beyond the left or right margin. | [M] | 1.2.25a, 4.4.18b |
| Caption: Calibri 11 pt, primary words capitalised. Number = part number (or chapter number if there are no parts), hyphen, sequence, in **bold**, then a tab, then the title in roman (not bold): "**Table 2-4**⇥Format for Reporting Overseas Events". See [A-23] for documents with neither parts nor chapters. | [M] | 1.2.25b, 4.4.11 |
| Table captions go **above** the table. Figure captions go **below** the figure. | [M] | 1.2.25b |
| Table content: Calibri 10 pt, 3 pt before and after. All borders 0.5 pt. | [M] | 1.2.25c |
| Header row: 11 pt (1.2.16(4)(b)). Table 1-2 shows it bold and centred; DFI templates show grey header shading, which is not stated in prose. See [A-24]. | [M]/[T] | Table 1-2 |
| Multi-page tables: repeat the header row (Word "repeat header rows") and repeat the caption with "(cont.)": "Table 2-4 Table Title (cont.)". | [M] | 1.2.25c |
| Cell alignment follows the content (left, centred or right as in Table 1-2). Bullets in tables: 10 pt dot, then diamond. | [T] | Table 1-2 |
| Graphics: only if relevant, purposeful and high quality. Keep 12 pt clear of other graphics and text. | [M] | 4.4.18b |
| Preferred image formats: colour high quality .tiff; colour low quality .jpg; text/line .emf; text and graphics .png; monochrome .png. PDF is acceptable. | [D] | Table 4-3 |
| Email attachments: photographs in .jpeg or .tiff only. | [M] | 2.1.8(6) |

## 2. Highlights: warnings, cautions, notes (4.4.20; publications)

| Highlight | Position | Font | Colour (RGB) | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Warning:** | **Before** the relevant paragraph, before any cautions | Calibri 12 pt, 1 cm tab, "Warning" bold, then a colon | 192, 0, 0 | [M] |
| **Caution:** | Before the relevant paragraph, after any warnings | Calibri 11 pt, 1 cm tab, "Caution" bold | 227, 108, 10 | [M] |
| **Note:** | **After** the relevant paragraph | Calibri 10 pt, 1 cm tab, "Note" bold | 0, 128, 0 | [M] |

- Spacing: 6 pt before and after, single line spacing. [M] 1.2.16(11)(g)
- Warnings and cautions must be self-contained (no references). Notes may
  contain references. None of them may contain procedural steps. [M]
- Selection criteria: warning = risk to life, of injury or long-term health
  hazard; caution = risk of equipment damage or destruction, or a security
  breach; note = additional important information. [M] 4.4.20e
- Highlights do not form part of the order, direction or instruction. [M] 4.4.20f
- DFI 5.1 itself also uses "Note:" and "Caution:" lines (smaller font) in
  correspondence-related prose (2.1.5, 2.1.19). The highlight format is defined
  only for publications. [T]
