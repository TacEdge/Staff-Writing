# 08 Authorised publications (DFO, DFI, DM)

Source: DFI 5.1 Part 4 (4.2.5, 4.2.7, 4.2.9–4.2.12, 4.3.10, 4.4.1–4.4.21,
Annexes 4A–4H). DFI 5.1 itself is the style exemplar (4.4.1b).

> DFOs are secondary legislation. Their drafting and punctuation follow the
> Parliamentary Counsel Office (4.3.14a, 4.1.9b), and their amendments follow
> PCO processes (4.3.10b). This repository can produce the DFO *layout* in
> Annexes 4A–4G, but it does not claim PCO conformance. See [A-37].

## 1. Structure (4.4.2–4.4.6)

| Division | Contents | Enforceable? | Page numbering (Restricted or below) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Introductory provisions | Title page (cover), authority order, foreword (optional), contents | No | Title page unnumbered. Lower-case Roman from "ii" on the authority order (4.4.7b(1) says it starts at "i"; the templates show "ii": see [A-36]). |
| Preliminary provisions | Purpose; application; commencement date; repeal; Authorising Officer, Approving Officer and Custodian (template order, Figs 4-7 to 4-9; the 4.4.4a prose lists the custodian before repeal); meanings of terms; authoritative version; related publications; Annex A Meanings of Terms (List of Abbreviations, Glossary) | Yes | Arabic: 1, 2, 3 |
| Main content | Parts → chapters → sections → cross headings → group-heading paragraphs; annexes after the relevant part, chapter or section; schedules at the end | Yes (schedules are enforceable) | part-page: 3-10 (annexes included in the sequence) |
| End matter | Record of change; enclosures | No | EM-1, EM-2 |

- Publications of 100 pages or more: parts → chapters → sections. Smaller ones:
  chapters → sections. [D] 4.4.1d
- Content order: substantive before procedural; general before particular;
  NZDF-wide before limited application. [D] 4.4.5b
- Classified publications (or any with a codeword or endorsement): number every
  page sequentially from "1" on the title page, per DFO 51. [M] 4.4.7c

## 2. Page format (4.4.7)

- Margins: top 3 cm, bottom 2.5 cm, left and right 2.5 cm. [M]
- Protective markings on all pages, including the covers. [M]
- Parts start on a new page. Chapter 2 and later chapters start on a new page.
  Section breaks separate parts. [M]
- The list of annexes at the end of a part, chapter or section is not preceded by
  a page break. [M]
- Blank pages are numbered. [M]

## 3. Header and footer (4.4.8)

Header, 1 cm from the top:

| Line | Left | Right |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Protective marking (centred, bold, upper case), where required | |
| 2 | Publication long title (sentence case), eg "DFI 5.1 Defence Force Writing" | Version number, eg "Version 2.01" |
| 3 | Part or chapter (sentence case), eg "Part 2 - Presentation of Defence Force Writing" | Commencement date dd Month yyyy, eg "03 October 2025" |

Footnote 98 ("if the publication is not separated into parts then leave this
line blank") is attached to the version-number item: see [A-25].

Footer, 1 cm from the bottom: page number centred, then the protective markings
centred, bold, upper case, on the last line.

## 4. Heading styles (4.4.11, 4.4.12)

| Style | Use | Format | Spacing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Title | Cover title | (title page template) | – |
| Heading 1 | Part: "PART 1 - TITLE" | UPPER CASE, Calibri 16 pt, bold, centred | 12/12 |
| Heading 2 | Chapter: "Chapter 2 - Title" | Primary Words Capitalised, 16 pt, bold, centred | 12/12 |
| Heading 3 | Section ("Section 1 - Title") and cross heading | Sentence case, 14 pt, **not bold, italic**, centred | 12/12 |

Note: DFI 5.1's own rendered pages do not visibly show Heading 3 in italic. The
prose (4.4.11) governs. Log any difference found when comparing pages.

## 7. Naming and numbering (4.2.7–4.2.12)

- On the title page and authority order, the full formal title is spelled out:
  "Defence Force Instruction 7.2" / "Official Information". In references the
  abbreviated form is used: DFO 10, DFI 7.2, DM 9.1-1, CDFGO, DAR 91. [M]
- DFO = first element (DFO ##). DFI = first.second (DFI ##.##). DM =
  first.second-third (DM ##.##-##). Optional suffixes: Level 1 extension (DFO 6-1);
  Command letter M/L/A/J (DFI 8.3M); classification identifier (TS)/(S)/(C)/(R).
  [M]
- MGR PIDS allocates numbers, with OCDF giving final approval. The renderer
  takes the number as input and validates its shape only. [M] 4.2.13

## 8. Cover, authority order and copyright

- Cover: release statement ("Releasable to the public." or "Not releasable to
  the public without command approval.") must be stated on the cover. [M] 4.2.4b
- Copyright notice on the front cover, verbatim [M] 4.2.5:
  > © Crown Copyright [year of publication]. This document is the property of
  > the New Zealand Defence Force. The text in this document may be reproduced
  > for use by members of the New Zealand Defence Force. Crown Copyright
  > material must not be used or reproduced for any other purpose without prior
  > permission of the Chief of Defence Force.
- Authority order: authority and conflict paragraphs, verbatim per Fig 4-4 (DFO)
  or Fig 4-5 (DFI/DM). The Authorising Authority signs and dates it. A major
  rewrite needs a new signed authority order. [M] 4.1.11b(1), fn 81

## 9. Amendments (4.3.10, 4.4.17)

- Revision bar: a vertical black line in the left margin beside changed, added
  or deleted text, tables or figures (not used for a major rewrite). [M]
- Version: X.YY. A minor amendment increments the decimal (1.00 → 1.01); a major
  amendment increments the whole number (1.00 → 2.00). The version and date
  appear on every page. [M]
- Deleted paragraph (minor amendment): keep the number and add
  "[n.n.n repealed on dd Month yyyy by …]" in 10 pt italic. [M]
- Inserted paragraph (minor amendment): previous number plus a capital letter
  (4.4.8A). [M]
- Record of change table columns: Amendment Number | Commencement Date |
  Reference | Details of Change | Approving Authority. The Details of Change
  vocabulary is: On Issue, Withdrawn, Repealed, Replaced, Inserted, Substitute.
  [M] Fig 4-11, End Matter
- New or amended publications are accompanied by the Publications QA Form
  (Annex 4H). [M] 4.3.12g

## 10. Preliminary-provision boilerplate

Figures 4-8 (DFO) and 4-9 (DFI/DM) hold fixed wording for application,
compliance, commencement, the authoritative version, and so on. Reproduce it
verbatim, with only the bracketed fields filled. Differences between the
templates: DFO application is "pursuant to s 27"; DFI/DM application is
"pursuant to s 30". The DFO authoritative version is the NZ Legislation website;
the DFI/DM authoritative version is the DIE.
