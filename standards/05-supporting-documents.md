# 05 Supporting documents: annexes, appendices, enclosures and flags

Source: DFI 5.1 1.2.24, Annex 1A Figs 1-4 to 1-6, 2.1.11(10) and (16),
2.1.16(18), 2.2.3(7), 2.3.8(10), 3.2.11(8), 4.4.7a(6).

## 1. Definitions and use

| Type | Definition | Identified by | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Annex** | Supplementary detail that only makes sense read with the parent document. | Capital letters (A, B, C) in order of first reference. Publications use part or chapter + letter (1A). | [M] 1.2.24(1), 4.4.7a(6) |
| **Appendix** | A stand-alone supplement that forms part of an annex. Must be referred to in the annex. | Arabic numerals, in order of reference within the annex. | [M] 1.2.24(2) |
| **Enclosure** | A complete document or extract sent with the main document. | Sequential numbers (1, 2), referenced by title and date. | [M] 1.2.24(3) |
| **Flag** | Self-adhesive marker on supporting material in briefs and DPBs (sparingly in minutes). | Letters (Flag A). Side flags: A1, A2. | [M] 1.2.24(4) |

Where supporting documents are permitted:

| Document | Annexes | Appendices | Enclosures | Flags | Cite |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Minute / submission | yes | yes | yes | sparingly | 2.1.11(16), 1.2.24(4) |
| Formal letter | **no** | no | yes (if relevant) | – | 2.1.16(18) |
| Administrative documentation | yes | yes | yes | DPB yes | 2.2.3(7), 2.2.7(4) |
| Visit report / PAR | yes; **Annex A must be the travel and cost summary** | yes | yes | – | 2.2.10(8)–(9) |
| Note to the Minister | not usually with a one-page note; allowed with full notes | yes | yes | – | 2.3.8(10) |
| CDF Directive | in exceptional circumstances | – | yes | – | 3.2.11(8) |
| CDF Operational Directive, AI | yes | – | yes | – | 3.2.18(8), 3.2.22a(8) |

## 2. Rules

| Rule | Tag | Cite |
|---|---|---|
| Introduce each supporting document in the text, in sequence. Wording: "is **in** Annex A" (not "at"); "Annex A refers"; "incorporated in Enclosure 2". A single enclosure may be described in the text ("the form entitled Leave Application is enclosed"). | [M] | 1.2.24(1), (5); 2.1.11(10) |
| A supporting document must not carry a protective marking higher than its parent. | [M] | 2.1.11(16), 2.3.8(10) |
| Supporting documents should be complete and able to stand alone. | [M] | Fig 2-3 para 5, 2.3.8(10), 4.4.6b |
| The final electronic version is one Word file (annexes, appendices and enclosures included). | [M] | 1.2.24(6) |
| Filing: keep supporting documents with the parent in the DIE (scan to PDF if needed). | [M] | 2.1.10c |

## 3. Listing at the end of the parent

| Rule | Tag | Cite |
|---|---|---|
| Annexes are listed after the signature block (or at the end of the relevant part, chapter or section in publications). The list is **not** preceded by a page break. | [M] | 1.2.24(1)(a) |
| Enclosures are listed after the annexes, by title and date. | [M] | 1.2.24(3) |
| A single enclosure is still listed, under the heading "Enclosure". | [M] | 1.2.24(5)(c) |
| List headings: templates use **Annex(es)** and **Enclosure(s)** in bold, items labelled "A." and "1.". See [A-27]. | [T] | Figs 2-3 to 2-6 |
| Order after the signature block: telephone line → Annex(es) → Enclosure(s) → (Flags, DPB) → Distribution → Copy Distribution → Ministerial referral (NTM) / Consultation (DPB). See [A-39] for the order of the DPB items. | [T]/[M] | Figs 1-4, 2-3, 2-17, 2-27; 1.2.16(9) |

## 4. Identifying block (top right of the first page)

| Rule | Tag | Cite |
|---|---|---|
| **Annex**: right-aligned, **bold upper case**, at the top right of the first page. Line 1: ANNEX [letter]. Line 2: the supporting document's unique identifier (if it has one). Line 3: the authorisation date if it has been formally authorised. With no unique identifier, only ANNEX G. | [M] | 1.2.24(1)(b)–(d) |
| The DFI examples show line 2 and line 3 in mixed case ("Organisation Detail", "CDF Directive 999/2021", "31 December 2021"; Fig 1-5 "[DD MMM YY]"). See [A-12]. | [A-12] | |
| Below the identifying block: the annex's own subject heading in **bold upper case** at the left margin. | [M] | 1.2.24(1)(e) |
| **Appendix**: same block style. Text: "APPENDIX 1 OF ANNEX A" (prose). Fig 1-6 shows "APPENDIX [n] TO ANNEX [X]". See [A-12]. | [M]/[A-12] | 1.2.24(2)(a) |
| **Enclosure**: may have an identifying block (bold upper case, top right). | [D] | 1.2.24(3) |
| **Publications**: "ANNEX 1A". Continuation pages: "ANNEX 1A (cont.)". Letters restart at A for each part or chapter. | [M] | 4.4.7a(6)(a) |
| Protective markings on annexes repeat in the header and footer of every page. | [M] | Figs 1-5, 1-6 |

## 5. Pagination of supporting documents

See `02-page-layout.md` §5. In summary: correspondence annexes A-1, A-2;
appendices A-1-1; single-page annexes and appendices are not numbered; classified
documents count all supporting pages in "Page n of N"; publications include
annexes in the part/chapter page sequence.

## 6. Flags (1.2.24(4))

- Identified alphabetically and introduced in **bold** in the text ("…is at
  **Flag A**"). Primary flags go along the top edge. Side flags (**Flag A1**) mark
  sections within flagged material and go along the side edge. [M]
- Note the preposition: flags use "at", annexes and enclosures use "in". [M]
- DPB lists flags after the enclosures, under the heading **Flags**, items "A.".
  [T] Fig 2-17
