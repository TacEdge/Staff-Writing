# 03 Headings, paragraph numbering and lists

Source: DFI 5.1 1.2.17, 1.2.23, 2.1.3(3)–(4), Annex 1A (Fig 1-4), 3.2.11(6),
3.2.18(6), 3.2.22a(7), 4.4.9–4.4.16.

## 1. Heading types (correspondence and administrative documents)

A heading is not part of the text. The body must make grammatical sense without
it. [M] 1.2.17

| Heading | Format | Tag | Cite |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Subject heading** | Required on every document except external letters (optional there). Left margin, **BOLD UPPER CASE**. Must be specific enough to find in DDMS. Notes to the Minister: no abbreviations. Annexes, appendices and enclosures each have their own subject heading. | [M] | 1.2.17(1), 1.2.9(6), 2.1.11(8), 2.3.8(7) |
| **Main heading** | Only for complex or lengthy documents. Introduces two or more paragraphs. **Bold, Primary Words Capitalised, centred.** Not numbered. | [M] | 1.2.17(2) |
| **Group heading** | Introduces one or more paragraphs up to the next group or main heading. **Bold sentence case**, left margin, **not numbered**. Proper nouns keep their capitals. | [M] | 1.2.17(3) |
| **Paragraph heading** | **Bold sentence case ending with a full stop**, run in before the paragraph text at that paragraph's tab stop. If any sub-paragraph under an introductory paragraph has one, they all must. | [M] | 1.2.17(4) |

Spacing: subject, main and group headings 12 pt before and 6 pt after
(02-page-layout §3).

## 2. Paragraph numbering schemes

DFI 5.1 has three distinct schemes. Every template selects exactly one of them.

### Scheme C: Correspondence (minutes, submissions, notes to the Minister, visit reports, DPBs, delegations; internal correspondence generally)

Source: 2.1.3(3), Annex 1A paras 6–7 (Fig 1-4 p2).

| Level | Label | Label position | Text starts | Turnover lines | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `1.` | left margin (0 cm) | 1 cm | **back to the left margin** (no hanging indent) | [M] 2.1.3(3); see [A-05] |
| 2 | `a.` | 1 cm | 2 cm | 2 cm (aligned under the first line of text) | [M] |
| 3 | `(1)` | 2 cm | 3 cm | 3 cm | [M] |
| 4 | `(a)` | 3 cm | 4 cm | 4 cm | [M] |

- Numbers run sequentially through the document in Arabic numerals followed by
  a full stop. [M]
- **If there is only one first-level paragraph, it is not numbered.** [M] 2.1.3(3)
- Numbering in internal emails is optional, but required if the email gives an
  order, direction or instruction. [M] 2.1.6f
- External correspondence: **no paragraph numbers**, all text at the left
  margin. [M] 2.1.3(4), 2.1.16(12)
- Paragraph grading indicator, when used: after the number, for example
  `2.(U)` or `2.(C)`. [D] 2.1.11(12)

### Scheme D: Directives, orders and instructions (CDF Directive, CDF Operational Directive, AI, DFO(T))

Source: fn 23, 3.2.11(6), 3.2.18(6), 3.2.22a(7), Figs 3-2 to 3-7.

| Level | Label | Label position | Text starts | Turnover lines | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `1.` | left margin | 1 cm | **1 cm (hanging)** | [M] |
| 2 | `a.` | 1 cm | 2 cm | 2 cm | [T] |
| 3 | `(1)` | 2 cm | 3 cm | 3 cm | [T] |
| 4 | `(a)` | 3 cm | 4 cm | 4 cm | [T] |

Delegations (2K–2N) and the MOU (2V) are also drawn with hanging first-level
paragraphs, although they are administrative documents. See [A-05].

### Scheme P: Authorised publications (DFO, DFI, DM)

Source: 4.4.13–4.4.16, Fig 4-10, and DFI 5.1's own pages.

| Element | Label | Label position | Text starts / wraps | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| First-level paragraph (group heading) | `1.2.3` (part.chapter.n) or `1.3` (chapter.n if there are no parts). The label is part of a Heading 4 style. | left margin | 2 cm (two tab stops) | [M] 4.4.14c |
| Second-level paragraph | `a.` (only if there is more than one; a single second-level paragraph is unnumbered) | left margin | 1 cm | [M] 4.4.15 |
| Sub-paragraph | `(1)` | 1 cm | 2 cm | [M] 4.4.16b(1) |
| Next | `(a)` | 2 cm | 3 cm | [M] |
| Next | `(i)` | 3 cm | 4 cm | [M] |
| Final (only if absolutely necessary) | `(A)` | 4 cm | 5 cm | [M] |
| Introductory provisions and end matter | `1.`, `a.` | | | [M] 4.4.13 (see [A-26]) |

- Do not divide below the fifth sub-paragraph level. [D] 4.4.16b
- The first word of sub-paragraph text is lower case unless it is a proper noun.
  [M] 4.4.16b(1)
- "Reserved" and "not allocated" paragraphs are not used. [M] 4.4.14c(2)
- Amendment numbering (4.4.8A; "[repealed …]" in 10 pt italic) is covered in
  `08-publications.md`.

## 3. Vertical lists (1.2.23)

| Rule | Tag |
|---|---|
| **Single-sentence list:** a lead-in stem; each item completes the sentence, is numbered, starts lower case (unless a proper noun), ends with a semicolon; the last ends with a full stop; the second-last adds "and" or "or" after the semicolon. | [M] |
| **Multi-sentence list:** introduced by a complete sentence; each item is one to three complete sentences, numbered, starts with a capital and ends with a full stop. | [M] |
| Lead-in punctuation: **colon** in correspondence and administrative documentation; **em dash** in orders, instructions and manuals. | [M] 1.2.23c |
| Hierarchical or ordered lists must be numbered. All list items in orders, directions and instructions must be numbered, so they can be referenced. | [M] 1.2.23d, f |
| No more than nine items, and avoid more than two levels. | [D] 1.2.23g(4) |

## 4. Bullets (1.2.23g, Annex 1A para 7, 2.2.7(2))

| Rule | Tag |
|---|---|
| **No bullets in formal letters, orders, directions or instructions** (including directives). They may be used in other Defence Force writing (administrative documentation, DPBs, email, tables). | [M] 1.2.23g, 2.1.16(12), 2.2.3(5) |
| Use bullets only for unordered lists that will not be referred back to. | [D] |
| First level: small round dot at the first tab (1 cm). Second level: small diamond aligned with the first-level text (second tab). No asterisks or other symbols. | [M] |
| DPB: text wraps under the first word of the bullet. The bullet is at the first tab and the text at 2 cm from the margin. | [M] 2.2.7(2) |
| Items start lower case (unless a proper noun) with open punctuation. Questions end with "?". | [M] |
| Table bullets: dot, then diamond, 10 pt. | [T] Table 1-2 |
