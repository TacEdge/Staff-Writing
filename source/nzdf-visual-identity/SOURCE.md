## Status in this repository

- **DFI 5.1 remains the only authority** for writing, formatting, structure and
  layout (CLAUDE.md §1). This document is used for two things only:
  1. **Artwork.** It is the source of the badge and logo artwork (register T-05).
  2. **Use of the logo and logotype.** DFI 5.1 defers to it for how the logo
     and the Force for New Zealand logotype are used together (1.2.26f: "as
     specified in the NZDF Visual Identity Standards").
- **Where it differs from DFI 5.1** on a document layout, DFI governs, and the
  difference is logged in `docs/ambiguities-and-decisions.md` (items BR-nn).
- **Possibly a different document from the one DFI names.** DFI cites "DFI 0.103
  NZDF Identity Standards" (1.1.3f, 4.1.7b) and "DFI 3.1 NZDF Identity Standards"
  (1.2.26). This document does not carry either reference (see register A-32).
- **Legal protection.** It states that NZDF logos and badges are protected by the
  Flags, Emblems, and Names Protection Act 1981 and must not be used in a way
  that falsely implies NZDF approval. It also says not to manipulate, recolour or
  recreate them.
