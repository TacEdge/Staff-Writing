## Reading the template figures

- In the template annexes, **red text marks placeholders** and black text is
  fixed wording.
- The figures are scaled down inside a frame. Measure geometry relative to the
  figure frame, not the DFI page.
- The black vertical bar in the DFI left margin is the DFI's own revision bar
  (4.3.10c(1)). It is not part of the template.
- DFI 5.1 itself is the exemplar for publication style (4.4.1b). Its own pages
  show the DFI/DM publication layout.
